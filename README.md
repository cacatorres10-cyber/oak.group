# OAK Group

One-page website for OAK Group: air conditioning across London and residential construction across East London and Essex. Based in West Horndon, Essex.

- `index.html`: the whole site (HTML, CSS and JS in one file)
- `assets/`: photos, hero video and logo files

The quote form at the bottom of the page writes a ready-made email to info@oakgroup.ltd in the visitor's email app. The visitor only has to press Send.

To preview, open `index.html` in a browser.
