One unit, all year. It heats your home in winter and cools it in summer.

A modern split air conditioner is a heat pump. In winter it pulls heat from the outside air, even on frosty mornings, and moves it into the room. In summer it does the reverse. For every unit of electricity it uses, it delivers roughly three units of heat, which is why it costs less to run than storage heaters or a fan heater.

We size every system from a survey of your rooms, fit it neatly, and service it every year after. Installation, heating, servicing and repairs, all from the same local team.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Can air conditioning heat my home in winter?

Yes. A split system works as an air to air heat pump. In heating mode it pulls heat from the outside air, even below freezing, and puts it into the room.

What is the difference between air to air and air source heat pumps?

Air to air heats the room directly through a wall-mounted unit, and cools in summer too. Air to water heats water for radiators or underfloor heating. We tell you at the survey which suits your home.

Can it replace my storage heaters?

Yes, and it is one of the best swaps you can make. Storage heaters charge overnight and often run out by the evening. A heat pump heats when you need it, using less electricity.

How long does an installation take?

Most single room installs are done in a day. Multi-room systems take longer, and you get the timeline with your quote.

How often should it be serviced?

Once a year for most homes. Busy commercial sites, like kitchens and pubs, can need more.

Do you service and repair systems you didn't fit?

Yes. We service and repair most makes. Add the make and model in the quote form if you have them.

Which areas do you cover?

Brentwood, Upminster, West Horndon, Hornchurch, Romford, Billericay and across Essex and East London. We are based in West Horndon.
