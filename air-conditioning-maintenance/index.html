Ongoing

Serviced units outlast neglected ones. And they cost less to run.

Dirty filters and coils make a system work harder for the same result. That shows up on the electricity bill, then as a breakdown in the middle of summer.

A yearly service stops that. We clean it, test it and fix small problems while they're still small. For businesses, a maintenance contract puts every unit on a schedule so nothing gets missed.

Servicing, Maintenance & Repairs

What a Service Covers

The same checklist on every visit, so nothing is left to memory.

01

Filters cleaned

Filters cleaned or replaced so air flows freely and the air in the room stays clean.

02

Coils and fans checked

Indoor and outdoor coils cleaned, fans checked for wear and balance.

03

Refrigerant and pressures

System pressures checked by a certified engineer, leaks traced if something's off.

04

Drain and condensate

Drain line cleared so there are no drips, smells or water damage.

05

Electrical checks

Connections and controls checked and tightened.

06

Performance test

Cooling and heating tested and logged, so you know it's running as it should.

Questions

Frequently Asked

How often should air conditioning be serviced?

Once a year for most homes. Busy commercial sites, like kitchens and pubs, can need more.

Do you service systems you didn't install?

Yes. We service and repair most makes, for homes and businesses.

My AC isn't cooling or heating properly. Can you fix it?

Usually, yes. Tell us what it's doing in the quote form, and add the make and model if you have them. We'll book a visit to find the cause.

What's a maintenance contract?

A scheduled service plan for one or more units. You don't have to remember to book, and faults get picked up early. It's popular with pubs, restaurants and offices.
